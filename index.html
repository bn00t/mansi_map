MANSI: the whole course on one page

The course makes one argument in five steps. Sustainability problems are systemic, so innovation has to reach the system (L1). To do that you need to understand how systems change and who holds power (L2), what digital technology does once people use it (L3), how impact can grow responsibly (L4), and who builds the ventures that carry it (L5). The exam asks one question per lecture, each with three parts: explain the concept, apply it to an organization, reflect on consequences.
